How PantryPal works

  1. Fill your pantry

    Add what you have, how much, and when it expires. Takes seconds per item.

  2. Get matched recipes

    Every recipe gets a match score based on your real quantities, not just ingredient names.

  3. Cook, plan, shop

    Plan your week and get a shopping list that skips what you already own.

A smart pantry that keeps count

Sort ingredients into fruits, dairy, grains and more. Grams, kilos, litres and pieces are converted automatically, so 1 kg of flour covers a recipe that needs 250 g.

Recipes ranked by what you own

Have 4 of the 5 ingredients for a vegetable pasta? That's an 80% match. Missing butter? PantryPal suggests olive oil, with the right amount, if it suits the dish.

Use it before you lose it

A background check scans your pantry every hour. Anything close to its date shows up under "Your Ingredients Need Some Love!", with recipes that use it.

A week of meals, one tidy list

Drop recipes into breakfast, lunch and dinner. The shopping list adds up every planned recipe and subtracts your stock, so you only buy what's missing.
